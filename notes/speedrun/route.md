# Dune (Cryo, 1992) speedrun: route transcription

Source: `~/Downloads/Dune-Speed-Run.mp4` (7193 s). Transcribed from 48 contact sheets taken every 5 s, plus single frames or 3x2 strips pulled with ffmpeg where a sheet was unclear. Timestamps are video seconds, accurate to about 5 s. Raw per-sheet notes are in `notes.txt` in this folder.

## Version

**Floppy (PC DOS) version.** Evidence:
- It starts from `C:\DUNE>` in DOS and uses floppy-style manual copy protection (a manual page/word check at about 40–45 s).
- All portraits are hand-drawn pixel art. There are no digitised faces and no speech (the CD version has video faces).
- The credits say "Adlib music uses HERAD" and "music available on CD edited by Virgin Music".
- It ends with "Coming soon: DUNE 2" and then `C:\DUNE>Thank you for watching! Long live the Duke`.

Place labels are copied exactly as the game shows them. In this build the Atreides home is labelled **"Palace: Carthag (Atreides)"** and the final target is **"Palace: Arrakeen (Harkonnen)"**.

## Overall shape of the run

The run finishes on **day 5, charisma 99**. The Water of Life is never drunk. Liet-Kynes is never met (he only appears in the end credits). The smugglers are visited but nothing is bought. Ecology is never used. The run is a race to take every Harkonnen fortress with Fremen troops. From day 4 it relies heavily on worm riding and on saving and reloading around battles.

## Ordered route

### Day 1: Palace and first sietches (0–300 s)
1. **0–35**: Launch from DOS, then the intro (Virgin, Cryo, space scenes). **40–45**: copy-protection page.
2. **55–75**: Paul's room, then the mirror/globe. First save: *Log1 DAY 1 7:30 am*.
3. **80**: Throne room. Talk to **Duke Leto** ("I'm the Duke Leto…"). He sets the spice/stillsuit quest (135).
4. **85–115**: Take the ornithopter with **Gurney** to nearby sietches. Messages: "My troop is settled in **Carthag-Tuek**", then "…in **Carthag-Harg**". These are the first Fremen troops.
5. **150–200**: Orni flights east (orni navigation "DESERT eastwards"). A Fremen leader talks about stillsuits and says "two more sietchs… north and east". A troop joins at **Tuono-Tabr** (200). The map shows **Carthag-Timin** (205).
6. **220–225**: A Fremen says his people are "unbeatable in spice prospecting" and offers to "update this map". This reveals sietches and spice.
7. **235–290**: Back at the palace. Leto (traps). **Jessica** shows the **hidden door** (245–255). Leto in the communication room congratulates Paul (270). Jessica says charisma is rising. Leto asks for a prospecting troop and says "stocks of spice reduced to zero" (290).
8. **300**: Save *DAY 1 7:30 pm*. Charisma 2.

### Day 2: Harah, the vision, and Gurney's disappearance (300–800 s)
9. **310–355**: Orni to **Carthag-Tabr**, then **Tuono-Harg**. The Fremen there name "Paul Atreides". The area must be prospected, so the player opens the spice-density map.
10. **370–395**: Communication room: **Emperor** asks for the first spice shipment. Leto points out the rich Fremen area at Tuono-Tabr. Rallied troops = 5.
11. **405–495**: Save *DAY 2*. Troops at Carthag-Tuek and others: 2430 men, set to **spice mining** ("on trial in spice"). One is sent to Carthag-Harg. **Harah** joins at a sietch (455–460). The Fremen agree "to let my troop work for you" (490).
12. **510–540 (Paul's vision trigger)**: Jessica: "walk out into the desert alone". Tell Harah **STAY HERE**. Walk out of the palace alone and choose **WAIT FOR EVENING** in the desert. Leto sends a telepathic message: "A message has arrived in the palace."
13. **545–600**: Communication room. **Send a spice shipment** to the Emperor (580–595). Talk to Emperor **Shaddam IV** (605).
14. **620–665 (Vision)**: Jessica: "Look! Paul is back!" Leto talks about "vision stuff". Everyone gathers on the balcony. Paul: "I can see dunes…". Harah: "Paul is the One". Leto: "Paul has visited 11 sietchs and 7 troops are with us". Jessica on Bene Gesserit powers and the vision.
15. **670–700**: Leto: "**Gurney Halleck has disappeared!**" Jessica asks Paul to find him. A body lies in the hall (685).
16. **710–750**: Leto sends Paul to **Thufir Hawat** in the communication room. Thufir goes along ("Ok let's go"). The **trap is removed and the armory is found** behind the door; a guard is posted. Harkonnens control part of the planet. Thufir says a Fremen leader is needed to rally troops.
17. **760–795**: Charisma 8. Harah: "Far west of palace a sietch, **Stilgar** nearby… I can lead you." Gurney is back in the party (795).

### Day 2–3: Stilgar and a military build-up (800–1300 s)
18. **815–870**: Orni to **Sihaya-Clam** and nearby. **Meet Stilgar** (860): "MUAD'DIB… our water shall mingle". Stilgar joins.
19. **890–945**: Troop at **Ergsun-Timin**, given **krys knives** (equipment). Troop sent to **Ergsun-Tabr** on **military training** (2350 men). Another is moved to **Bledan-Harg** on **espionage**. Rallied 8, *DAY 3 3:00 am*, charisma 19.
20. **955–1190**: Recruit at **Ergsun-Clam** (military, 960 men), **Ergsun-Harg** (military, 2140 men) and **Ergsun-Tsymyn** (military, 1070 men). An old leader says he was convinced by Stilgar to join. Rallied 11, then 12. Charisma 21.
21. **1200–1345**: Espionage reveals **Fort Bledan-Harg** (first Harkonnen fortress). Troops on military training at **Tuono-Clam**, **Tuono-Tuek** (2350 men) and **Tuono-Tabr** (1740 men, army). Rallied 13. Save *DAY 3 4:30 pm*.

### Day 3 evening: Smugglers, Harah leaves, Chani (1350–1800 s)
22. **1365–1395**: Orni to a **smugglers' village**. The smuggler offers an orni for 1500 kg of spice. The player chooses **STOP TALKING** and buys nothing. **Harah leaves** ("tired of travels… back to Tuono-Timin").
23. **1440–1445**: Stilgar: "I'd like you to meet somebody important… the village on your map."
24. **1460–1600**: **Habbanya-Timin**. Flight with Stilgar. A second orni is at a sietch. Visit a pink-domed village (equipment location). Save *DAY 3 10:30 pm*.
25. **1690–1745 (Chani)**: *DAY 4 3:00 am*. At a sietch at night: **Chani** ("I've heard about you, Paul"). WAIT FOR MORNING, then the Chani love scene ("red nights"). Chani joins. Charisma 26.

### Day 4 morning: First fort taken and Leto's death (1800–2200 s)
26. **1800–1920**: Troops attack Fort Bledan-Harg. **Oxtyn-Harg** troop on military training (2410 men). Jessica sends a telepathic message. **1910–1920 milestone**: "We won the battle Muad'Dib, here in **Bledan-Harg**! Yaoouuuh!" This is the **first fort taken (day 4, charisma 29)**.
27. **1995–2015**: Carthag-Harg troop has done spice mining for half a day. The player uses **CHANGE TROOP OCCUPATION** (menu: specialize in spice / army / ecology, go and search for equipment, espionage) to switch it to military.
28. **2075–2100 (Leto dies)**: *DAY 4 9:00 am*. Jessica: "PAUL! … **The Duke is dead.** He was killed by the Harkonnens. Thufir will give you the details." "These Harkonnen are beasts."
    - Quirk: at 2105–2115 the throne room still lists DUKE LETO ATREIDES and he says "Keep on going Paul!". This happens again at 6265–6295.
29. **2130–2170**: **Duncan Idaho** now holds the throne room. Thufir: attack the remote Harkonnen fortresses; the Duke's transport ornithopters were shot down; find another way to travel, as the Fremen do. Stilgar: worms. Chani: "you can try to ride the Worm".

### Day 4: Worm riding and the fortress campaign (2200–5050 s)
30. **2195–2215 (first worm ride)**: In the desert choose **CALL A WORM** and ride to the palace. Stilgar: "you have now **perfected the riding of the worm**". From then on, maps offer **GO THERE RIDING A WORM**. Charisma 49.
31. **2290–2330**: Orders to troops. **Arrakeen-Harg** troop is set to **attack**. Gurney rejoins (2385). Ride a worm to **Village Oxtyn-Pyons** (2395).
32. **2430–2560**: The attack on **Fort Arrakeen-Harg** starts (1500 men). **Arrakeen-Clam** is on espionage. Contacting far troops fails with "TOO FAR TO BE CONTACTED (Paul's powers too low)".
33. **2600–2955: Fort Arrakeen-Harg.** Ride a worm to the battlefield. The battlefield menu offers MASSIVE ATTACK / FIGHT FOR A WHOLE DAY / CALL A WORM. Stilgar comments ("forces much stronger", "smoke screen", "massive attack is hazardous but quick"). Saves are loaded again and again until the kill count rises (960, then 1440 killed). The captured fort becomes a military-training sietch. Stilgar: "Good to see these Harkonnen are beaten off!" Rallied 16, charisma 52.
34. **2985–3295: Fort Arrakeen-Clam.** Worm there. The **Harkonnen Captain** inside says "Come closer you miserable rat…". Troops are given **atomics** from the fort's stock ("Oh my God… Atomics…!?"). Freed prisoners: "We were captured by the Harkonnen troops here…". A fort becomes a sietch ("transforming this ugly fortress into a nice sietch"). Rallied 17, charisma 57.
35. **3315–3345**: **Gurney stays in a captured fort to train the Fremen** ("teach these Fremen the handling of arms"). The captured Captain, spared, reveals **Fort Arrakeen-Timin**.
36. **3450–3740: Forts Bledan-Tuek, Tsympo-Siet, Arrakeen-Siet and Arrakeen-Tuek** are taken the same way. Rallied 18–20. Save *DAY 4 6:00 pm*, charisma 59–62.
37. **3770–4005: Fort Arrakeen-Tsymyn**, attacked at 95% motivation. Freed slave-Fremen join ("compelled to work for the Harkonnen"). A Captain offers information in exchange for his life. Rallied 22.
38. **4050–4195**: **Weirding modules** and **laser guns** are handed out from fort equipment. Espionage troops go to **Tsympo-Tabr**, **Tsympo-Clam** and **Tsympo-Pyort**. Charisma 67, *DAY 4 7:30 pm*.
39. **4200–4495: Forts Tsympo-Pyort and Arrakeen-Timin.** A new leader joins ("If you've managed to convince Stilgar…"). A captain says **Fort Haga-Harg** is "heavily defended". Rallied 23, charisma 72.
40. **4500–4945: Forts Tsympo-Clam and Tsympo-Timin.** A telepathic message says **Bledan-Tuek** was won (4815). A captain says "There are 2 troops there". A chief says a sietch lies to the south. Rallied 24–25, charisma 76.
41. **4995–5055: Fort Tsympo-Tabr**, reached by worm: 2040 Harkonnens killed with no losses. **Day 5 begins** (*DAY 5 12:00 am*, charisma 79).

### Day 5: The last fortresses (5050–6150 s)
42. **5100–5400**: Tsympo-Tabr becomes a sietch. A troop goes to **Carthag-Harg** for military training. Night ornithopter hops between forts to redistribute equipment. A telepathic message says **Bledan-Timin** was won (5370). Rallied 27, charisma 83.
43. **5390–5545: Fort Haga-Harg** is taken, then **Fort Bledan-Tabr**. A captain: "In this area there are no fortresses that you have not already located."
44. **5550–5840: Fort Haga-Tsymyn** is taken. The worm menu offers BACK TO STARTING POINT / TOWARDS NEAREST PLACE / CHANGE DESTINATION. Rallied 28–30, charisma 91.
45. **5850–6090: Fort Haga-Siet** is taken. Telepathic messages say **Bledan-Tabr** and **Haga-Tuek** were won. Charisma 95.
46. **6095–6105 milestone**: Stilgar: "There are **no more Harkonnen fortresses** on Dune. The Arrakeen palace is the only Harkonnen place remaining. To attack the palace, I suggest that we use the help of **Thufir Hawat**… ask Thufir to come with us to one of the places around the palace." Charisma 97.

### Day 5 evening: Gathering the party and the palace assault (6150–6900 s)
47. **6180–6215**: Orni to the Atreides palace. **Thufir** is in the communication room: "Did you show this greenhouse to Chani and Stilgar?", "We now control more areas than the Harkonnen do", "Ok, let's go!" **Thufir joins.** Thufir: "Let's go to one of the sietchs close to the Arrakeen palace."
48. **6235–6260**: Mirror, then Paul's bedroom. **Jessica joins.**
49. **6355–6395**: At a captured fort: Gurney: "I understand the final attack is imminent", "OK!" **Gurney joins.**
50. **6445–6460**: At the cave sietch: Stilgar: "Yes, Muad'Dib, I'm with you". Chani: "I want to follow you, now and always". **Stilgar and Chani join.**
51. **6475–6570 (war council)**: Orni at evening to a fort-sietch next to the palace (*DAY 5 9:00 pm*). There is a scripted party conversation. Thufir: the palace is protected by a very powerful **shield**, and they need **at least 10000 men split across the three sietchs around the palace, each troop equipped with atomics**.
52. **6600–6640**: Give **atomics** to the Arrakeen-Tabr and Arrakeen-Timin troops (both on military training). Thufir: "We have enough men around the palace to attack it. You and Stilgar should launch the attack now."
53. **6660–6800 (palace assault)**: Stilgar: "troops are converging on the palace… wait until our troops put this shield down with atomics". Troops go to **Palace: Arrakeen (Harkonnen)** with orders to attack (1970 men at 65%, then 2070 men at 100%). A troop says "We're about to blow this shield out". Ride a worm to the palace battlefield. Chani: "If these Harkonnen capture you, you'll die." Saves are reloaded around the battle (*DAY 5 9:00 pm*).
54. **6835–6900**: The palace falls ("transforming this ugly fortress…"). The globe shows **Harkonnen men = 00** and Atreides men 57330. **Day 5, charisma 99.**

### Ending (6915–7193 s)
55. **6915**: Take the orni to the Harkonnen palace exterior. Gurney: "Look Paul! The Emperor is here!"
56. **6925–7050 (final cutscene)**: Throne room with the **Emperor Shaddam IV, the Baron, Feyd-Rautha**, and the party. Duncan (from his portrait): "You, Emperor Shaddam, are a liar…". Thufir: "machiavellian plan… Paul has upset your plan by rallying all of the Fremen". Stilgar to the Baron: "…mistreated our people". The Baron: "These Fremen, they're just a bunch of…". Jessica: "Paul is the One… the legend comes true". Feyd: "Don't make me LAUGH! He's just a WIMP!" Stilgar: "You had better not say that of your new Emperor!" The Emperor: "WHAT?!" Thufir: "the three of you are going to be exiled on the prison planet". Jessica: "**Long live the Emperor Paul Atreides and Chani his Empress**."
57. **7055–7190**: THE END, the character credits, Cryo/Virgin credits, and "Coming soon: DUNE 2". Back to DOS.

## Items and equipment seen
- A map update (sietches and spice) from the prospecting Fremen, day 1.
- Krys knives (day 3).
- The armory behind the trapped door (with Thufir, day 2).
- From captured Harkonnen forts: **atomics**, **weirding modules**, **laser guns**, and harvesters or ornithopters parked at forts.
- No ornithopter bought from the smugglers, no Water of Life, no ecology/bulb items.

## Mechanics the run depends on
1. **Dialogue triggers**: Leto, Jessica, Thufir, Gurney, Duncan, the Emperor, Harah, Stilgar, Chani, Fremen chiefs, Harkonnen captains, and the smuggler. This includes the party commands COME WITH ME / STAY HERE and the phase flags (hidden door, armory trap, Gurney disappearing, Leto's death, "no more fortresses", war council).
2. **The walk-into-the-desert vision** (WAIT FOR EVENING alone) and the balcony vision scene.
3. **Emperor spice shipments** in the communication room.
4. **Ornithopter travel** with the map, the in-flight nav screen (SKIP TO DESTINATION / CHANGE DESTINATION) and "GO THERE FLYING AN ORNI".
5. **Recruiting Fremen troops** at sietches, gated by charisma. The rallied-troop count and the contact range ("Too far to be contacted, Paul's powers too low") both depend on charisma.
6. **Troop orders**: change occupation (spice / army / ecology / espionage / search for equipment), move troop, modify equipment, and ask for more information. Troop stats (men, motivation, skill level) and "Settled in / Going to" labels.
7. **Spice density and prospecting maps**; **espionage revealing forts** and troop counts.
8. **Worm riding**: CALL A WORM, "GO THERE RIDING A WORM", and on arrival BACK TO STARTING POINT / TOWARDS NEAREST PLACE. It is unlocked after Leto dies.
9. **Attacking Harkonnen fortresses**: battle simulation with kill and loss counts, motivation boosted by Paul's presence, the MASSIVE ATTACK / FIGHT FOR A WHOLE DAY battlefield menu, and captured forts turning into sietches.
10. **Capturing forts**: freed prisoners and slaves join, Harkonnen captains can be spared for information, and fort equipment (atomics, weirding modules, laser guns) can be given to troops.
11. **Telepathic battle reports** ("We won the battle Muad'Dib, here in X!").
12. **Taking the Harkonnen palace**: the shield has to be removed by troops equipped with atomics in the three sietchs next to the palace (at least 10000 men), then the attack is launched, followed by the ending cutscene.
13. **Save and load** (two log slots plus "last entering into a place" / "last entering new sietch"), used a lot to reroll battle results.
14. **Game-clock progression** (day/hour display, "Nth day on DUNE" results screen, charisma) and **WAIT FOR EVENING / MORNING**.

Not needed: smuggler trading, Water of Life, Liet-Kynes, ecology/greening, ornithopter purchase.

## Uncertainties
- Some fort names in the 5-second samples were small. Tsympo and Arrakeen sub-names (Siet, Tuek, Tsymyn) came from zoomed frames, but the order within day 4 is approximate.
- The exact prompt at the copy-protection screen and the first sietch sequence (85–230 s) are brief. The troop names there (Carthag-Tuek, Carthag-Harg, Tuono-Tabr, Carthag-Timin) are taken from on-screen messages.
- The speaker who calls the Emperor a liar is identified from his portrait as Duncan Idaho (black uniform); Leto's portrait is similar.
